# 📋 Clipboard History Manager - LEIN

## Overview

**"Never lose what you've copied again."**

Clipboard History Manager is a lightweight Android utility app that automatically saves your copied text and lets you recall it instantly.  
All data is **stored locally on your device** — no internet required, no cloud, and absolutely no tracking.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Auto-Save** — Automatically records text the moment you copy it.
- **One-Tap Copy** — Tap any entry in your history to re-copy it immediately.
- **Pinning** — Fix your most-used clips to the top of the list.
- **Search** — Instantly find past entries using keywords.
- **Long-Press to Delete** — Easily remove individual history items.
- **Bulk Delete** — Clear all history (except pinned items) with a single tap.
- **100% Offline** — Everything stays on your device; your data never leaves your phone.
